Q: What pathway has the most significant “Entities p-value”? Do the most significant pathways listed match your previous KEGG results? What factors could cause differences between the two methods?

The most significant pathway is the one at the top of the Reactome results table (smallest Entities p‑value). It may not match the KEGG results exactly because KEGG and Reactome use different pathway definitions, gene sets, and statistical methods, which can lead to differences in significance rankings.
